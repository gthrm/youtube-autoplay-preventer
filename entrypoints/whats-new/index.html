✦ What's New

Previews stay quiet
in the sidebar too

Users reported that hover previews were blocked on the homepage but still played in the recommendations sidebar on watch pages. This release fixes that.

📺

Sidebar previews blocked Fixed

YouTube uses different markup for the "Up next" and recommendations column than for the homepage grid, so hovering there still started a preview. The blocker now covers that markup — including YouTube's newer view-model components — and pauses any preview player that slips through.

🖱️

Block autoplay on hover

Stops video thumbnails from auto-playing a preview clip when you hover over them — on the homepage, in search results, on channel pages and now in the watch page sidebar.

🚫

Block autoplay after video

YouTube silently navigates to the next recommended video the moment yours finishes. This toggle intercepts that navigation and stops it, so you stay in control of what you watch next. Enable it from the extension icon in your toolbar.
